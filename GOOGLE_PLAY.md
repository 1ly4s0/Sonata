### Full Description

**Sonata** is a beautiful, fast and lightweight local music player for Android.
Play your music entirely offline — no accounts, no subscriptions, no internet needed.

**🎵 All audio formats**
Sonata plays MP3, FLAC, AAC, M4A, OGG, OPUS, WAV, WMA, and more — anything stored on your device.

**▶️ Background playback**
Music keeps playing even when your screen turns off or when you switch to other apps. Perfect for workouts, commutes, and just living life.

**🔔 Lock screen & notification controls**
Full media session controls right from your lock screen or notification shade — play, pause, skip, seek.

**📂 "Open with" from any file manager**
Tap any audio file in your file manager, browser, or email and choose Sonata to play it instantly.

**📚 Smart library**
Your music is automatically organized by Songs, Artists, Albums, and Folders. No manual tagging needed.

**🔍 Fast search**
Find any song instantly by title, artist, or album name.

**❤️ Favorites & Playlists**
Mark your favorite tracks and create custom playlists. Your data is saved locally and never uploaded.

**🕓 Recently Played**
Quickly jump back to what you were listening to last.

**🔀 Shuffle & Repeat**
Shuffle all, repeat one track, or repeat the whole queue.

**🎨 Beautiful design**
Clean white library with bold orange-red accents. Dark immersive now-playing screen with album artwork.

**✅ No ads. No tracking. No internet permission required for playback.**
